Last session you vibe coded something in an unfamiliar stack. Before we discuss a framework for agentic coding, share what you made and compare notes.

## How this works

Get into **groups of 3 to 4**. You'll have **20 minutes** for the whole round, so each person gets about **5 minutes**. Keep an eye on the clock and pass the turn so everyone gets a fair share.

When it's your turn:

1. **Show your game and let people play it.** Pass it around or share your screen so the group actually gets their hands on it.
2. **Answer the three questions below out loud**, then open it up to the group.

## The questions

Work through these for your own project.

> - **Better or worse than you expected?** Where did the agent land against what you predicted going in?
> - **What went well, and what went badly?** Name the specific capabilities and the specific limits you ran into.
> - **What did you try, and what would you do differently?** How did you direct the agent? What would you change next time to direct it better?

## As a group

After each person shares, discuss.

> **With your group:** As you go, watch for overlap. Did the same kinds of tasks go smoothly for everyone? Did the agent break down in similar places across very different games? Try to name one capability and one limit that showed up in more than one person's project.
